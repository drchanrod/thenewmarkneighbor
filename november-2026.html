Association news

Annual HOA meeting

Sunday, November 8 at 7 p.m. · New Mark Clubhouse

Homeowners will elect board members at the November annual meeting. The HOA roster lists Mike Thomas, Paul Hinson, Debbie Mitchell, Robyn Dunlap, James Miller, and Chanel Rodriguez with terms ending in 2026.

HOA calendar · Board roster

Proposed one-time transfer fee

Homeowners will consider a proposal to establish a one-time transfer fee. Under the proposal, buyers would pay the fee at closing when they purchase a home in New Mark. Current homeowners would pay no fee on the homes they already own. The money would go into a Capital Improvement Account to build reserves for major neighborhood projects.

Watch your mail for 2027 dues invoices

The HOA will mail 2027 dues invoices in November. Dues are $500 for the 2027 calendar year. Please pay before February 1, 2027. If you need a payment plan, reach out to HOA President Mike Thomas or Treasurer Quinn Gregg.

October 24 · At the clubhouse

Fall Festival in photos

Neighbors of all ages brought their fall and Halloween spirit to the clubhouse on October 24. A little firefighter, a bright green dinosaur, and a crew of Minions took turns on the red carpet. Families bounced, played yard games, and browsed local artisan booths between bites from KC Hot Dog King and The Good Stuff Truck. At trunk-or-treat, an inflatable T. rex towered over candy buckets and a pickup bed became a haunted display.

Thank you to the HOA for sponsoring the festival and to Chanel Rodriguez of the MOJO Real Estate Team for sponsoring the costume contest and trunk-or-treat. We appreciate everyone who decorated a trunk, dressed up, volunteered, or came out to cheer. Congratulations to the contest winners!

Neighborhood tradition

Halloween in New Mark

Halloween turnout can vary wildly from one New Mark street to the next, but there were plenty of treats for kids and adults alike. We welcomed trick-or-treaters from around the neighborhood and even from other towns. Thanks to everyone who turned on a porch light, decorated, or took a moment to welcome a visitor. That’s one of the things we love about New Mark.

Todd and Lorraine Arbuckle’s Halloween House made it onto KMBC 9 News this year. Thanks to the Arbuckles for sharing a neighborhood tradition with the rest of Kansas City!

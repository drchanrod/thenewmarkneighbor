School is back in session

Slow down for bus stops and walkers.

New Mark Middle, Gateway 6th Grade Center, Bell Prairie, and Staley all have different start and stop times, which means students may be waiting for buses or walking through the neighborhood across a wide stretch of the day.

Please slow down, watch intersections and curves, and give kids extra room in the mornings and afternoons.

Neighborhood cleanup

Household dumpster day is September 19.

The HOA will provide one dumpster in the clubhouse parking lot for New Mark Brooking First Pool Association members in good standing to dispose of common household waste.

To use the dumpster, bring your current pool pass. Once the dumpster is full, nothing else will be accepted. Please form a single line on the south side of the parking lot so pool access is not blocked, and do not leave anything in the lot after the dumpster is removed.

Rick Head expects to be at the clubhouse by 8:00 A.M. and will post the expected arrival time after he is notified. The dumpster will likely be available from 9:00 A.M. to noon. Watch Facebook for final details closer to the date.

Trees, leaves & storm drains

Leaves are coming. Let’s keep storm drains clear.

It is still hot now, but fall cleanup season will be here before we know it. Please keep leaves and grass clippings out of the road so they do not wash into storm drains and contribute to street flooding.

Yard debris can also build up as sediment in the neighborhood pond over time, which can turn into a major expense when dredging is needed.

Northland leaf and brush day

Monday, October 26 is Leaf and Brush Day for the North. Check KCMO’s page for set-out rules, timing, and accepted materials before you haul everything to the curb.

See KCMO leaf and brush info

Check tree health now

Late summer is a good time to look for dead limbs, storm damage, branches over sidewalks or utilities, and trees that may need attention before fall storms arrive.

Public right-of-way trees

The tree lawn between the sidewalk and curb, medians, boulevards, and some sidewalk-less street edges may be public right-of-way. If a right-of-way tree needs attention, report it through the MyKCMO app or by calling 311.

Lend a hand in the garden

The community garden is still a shared summer spot for vegetables, herbs, flowers, and neighborly surprises. If you stop by, please pull a few weeds while you are there so the garden stays healthy through the heat.

Campbell Drive speed humps

Help us move the petition forward.

Speeding and cut-through traffic on Campbell Drive remain neighborhood concerns. Neighboring subdivisions have had speed humps installed, and residents have seen how much they can help slow traffic on through streets.

Only residents or property owners inside the city-defined petition area may sign, and only one signature is accepted per household. If you support the project and may be eligible, contact Robyn Dunlap at 816-835-6739.
